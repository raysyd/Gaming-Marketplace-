# Listing photos

Drop image files in this folder and they show up on the site immediately.

The demo listings look for these filenames:

    l-001.jpg   RTX 4080 Super prebuilt
    l-002.jpg   RTX 4090 Founders Edition
    l-003.jpg   Ryzen 9 7950X3D
    l-004.jpg   RTX 3060 starter build
    l-005.jpg   ASUS ROG Swift OLED monitor
    l-006.jpg   RX 7900 XTX
    l-007.jpg   Razer Blade 16
    l-008.jpg   Wooting 60HE keyboard
    l-009.jpg   PlayStation 5 Slim
    l-010.jpg   ITX RTX 4070 Ti build
    l-011.jpg   RTX 3080 Founders Edition
    l-012.jpg   G Pro X Superlight 2

Extra angles work too — `l-002-2.jpg`, `l-002-3.jpg` and so on appear as
gallery thumbnails. Anything missing falls back to the generated artwork, so
you can add photos one at a time without breaking the site.

Roughly 1200x900, under 300KB each, is plenty.

## Where to get them legitimately

Photograph the actual hardware. On a live marketplace that's the only option
that works anyway — buyers of used gear want to see the unit they're buying,
serial number and all, not a press render. Manufacturer press images are
copyrighted and misrepresent condition.

For a pitch demo, your own gear, a friend's rig, or a genuinely
license-free source (Unsplash, Pexels) will do.
